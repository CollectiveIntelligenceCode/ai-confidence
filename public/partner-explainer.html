The Key Point

No extra work. No merged CRMs.

— Each partner keeps their own CRM exactly as it is. Collective Intelligence contacts stay in Collective Intelligence. Solved Together clients stay in Solved Together.
— AI Confidence has its own mailing list — separate, clean, opt-in only. It lives in Resend. Nobody has to log into a new CRM or change how they work day-to-day.
— Enquiries from the website go into a shared HubSpot — but only AI Confidence leads. Not Solved Together clients, not Collective Intelligence clients. No overlap, no confusion.
— One opt-in email per partner is the only action required. After that, the list grows organically through content, events, and the website.
Day to day
Daily Operations

How this works in practice — every day.

The concern is a fair one: if we each have our own CRM and there is also an AI Confidence CRM, does that mean three times the admin? No. Here is exactly what each of us does day to day — and how little the AI Confidence side actually requires.

Chris — daily
Collective Intelligence CRM
Client meetings, proposals, follow-ups, invoices — all in the Collective Intelligence CRM as normal. Nothing changes. AI Confidence leads only appear here if Chris decides to cross-reference them, which is optional.
Rujuta — daily
Solved Together CRM (HubSpot)
Enterprise clients, transformation programmes, Solved Together pipeline — all in her own HubSpot as normal. She does not need to log into the AI Confidence HubSpot unless she is personally following up on an AI Confidence enquiry.
Lukáš — daily
His own tools
Facilitation clients, innovation projects — in whatever tools he already uses. Same as above: AI Confidence is a separate lane that does not touch his day-to-day unless he is handling an AI Confidence conversation specifically.

The only trigger for the AI Confidence CRM

01
Someone submits the form on aiconfidence.co
Automatic. The website creates a HubSpot contact and emails a notification to chris@collectiveintelligence.co. No one has to check a dashboard.
02
Chris gets the email and decides the next step
Is this a CXO who should hear from Rujuta? A consultant who needs Chris? An organisation Lukáš should talk to? Chris routes it to the right person. That conversation can happen over email — the CRM is just the record of it.
03
Whoever handles the lead logs the outcome in HubSpot
One note. One status update. Two minutes. This gives all three partners visibility on where every AI Confidence lead stands — without anyone having to ask.
04
That is it. Back to your normal day.
The AI Confidence CRM is not a place any of us lives in. It is a shared record that means no lead falls through the cracks and no partner is left wondering what happened to a conversation someone else had.

The worry is usually that a second CRM means double the admin. In practice it means about two minutes per lead — and only when a lead arrives. On quiet weeks, nobody opens it.
